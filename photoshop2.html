Photoshop Project 2

Advanced Retouching | Skin Texture | High-Fashion Aesthetics

The Brief: Creating high-fidelity beauty imagery that meets the standards of global fashion houses.


The Process: This showcase focuses on high-end retouching and "mirror-grade" craftsmanship. This approach is designed to resonate with high-end, instagrammable audiences and premium brands.
