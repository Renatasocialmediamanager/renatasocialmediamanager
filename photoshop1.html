Photoshop Project 1

Adobe Photoshop | Digital Compositing | Color Grading

The Brief: Creating "instagrammable" food content that emphasizes high-end aesthetics and cinematic storytelling.


The Process: This project involved advanced digital compositing to enhance texture and lighting. Utilizing "mirror-grade" attention to detail, I applied custom color grading to evoke a warm, luxury atmosphere. The goal was to create a raw yet polished visual that resonates with high-fashion brand standards like Zimmermann or Dior.
