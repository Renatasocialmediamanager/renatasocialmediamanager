The Power of Color

Hue
The actual color family or the name of the color (e.g., Red, Blue, Pink).
Saturation
The intensity or purity of the color. High saturation is vivid, while low saturation is muted or grayish.
Value
The lightness or darkness of a color, ranging from white to black.
