Choosing the Right Voice

By Renata Oliveira

A Social Media Manager's Guide to Font Types

In social media management, a font isn't just a letter; it's an emotion. Choosing the wrong font type can confuse your audience and dilute your brand message. Here are the four primary categories.

01. Serif Fonts

The Classic Elegance

Description: Serif fonts feature small decorative strokes at the ends of the letters. They are associated with tradition, reliability, and high-end luxury.

Best for: Editorial content, law firms, or luxury fashion brands (like Tiffany & Co.).

View Playfair Display

02. Sans Serif Fonts

Modern & Clean

Description: "Sans" means "without." These fonts lack decorative feet, offering a clean, minimalist, and approachable look.

Best for: Tech companies, modern startups, and mobile-first social media content.

View Montserrat

03. Script Fonts

Personal & Artistic

Description: These fonts mimic human handwriting or calligraphy. They add a personal, feminine, or creative touch to a design.

Best for: Logos, beauty brands, or "lifestyle" influencer content.

View Dancing Script

04. Monospaced Fonts

Technical & Precise

Description: Every character takes up the exact same amount of horizontal space. This creates a "typewriter" or "coding" aesthetic.

Best for: Displaying code, technical documentation, or retro-tech aesthetics.
