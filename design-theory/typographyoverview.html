The Art of Typography

The Silent Voice of Branding

Typography is the technique of arranging type to make language legible and visually appealing. It is a critical tool for establishing a brand’s hierarchy and personality.

01. Typeface
The creative design of the letters (e.g., Playfair Display).
02. Font
The specific style or size of that typeface (e.g., Bold, 12pt).
03. Serif
Small decorative "feet" at the ends of character lines.
04. Sans Serif
Clean typefaces without decorative strokes.
