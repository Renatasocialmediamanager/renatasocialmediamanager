The Art of Typography

The Silent Voice of Branding

Typography is the art of arranging type to make language legible and visually appealing. In digital branding, it is a critical tool for establishing hierarchy and personality.

01. Typeface
The creative design of the letters (e.g., Playfair Display).
02. Font
The specific style or size of that typeface (e.g., Bold, 12pt).
03. Leading
The vertical distance between lines of text.
