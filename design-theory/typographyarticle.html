The Psychology of Type

In digital design, typography is the silent ambassador of your brand. It communicates personality, authority, and tone before a single word is even read. For my personal brand, the choice of type reflects a balance between editorial luxury and modern digital efficiency.

"Typography is not just about choosing fonts; it’s about creating a visual voice that resonates with your audience's expectations."

The Luxury of the Serif

I use Playfair Display for my primary headings. As a Transitional Serif font, it carries a sense of timeless elegance and high-fashion "Vogue-style" sophistication. It tells my audience that they are entering a space of curated, luxury experiences.

The Modernity of Sans Serif

For body copy and navigation, I rely on Montserrat. This geometric sans-serif font is inspired by urban typography from the early 20th century. It provides the clean, functional "digital explorer" vibe that makes long-form travel articles easy to read on mobile devices.

Establishing Hierarchy

By contrasting the ornate nature of Serifs with the minimalist nature of Sans Serifs, I create a clear visual hierarchy. This guides my followers through the content in a way that feels organized and effortless.
