The Power of Color

Strategic Visual Psychology

Color is more than just an aesthetic choice; it is a psychological tool used to evoke emotion and drive user behavior. In my branding work, I use color to establish trust, energy, and luxury.

Hue
The actual color or name of the family (e.g., Red, Blue).
Saturation
The intensity or purity of a color. High saturation is vivid; low is muted.
Value
The lightness or darkness of a color (tints and shades).
Monochromatic
A scheme using different values of a single hue for a sophisticated look.
