The Art of Typography

The Silent Voice of Branding

01. Typeface
The creative design of the letters (e.g., Helvetica or Playfair Display).
02. Font
The specific style or size of that typeface (e.g., Playfair Display Bold, 12pt).
03. Serif
The small decorative "feet" at the ends of character lines.
04. Sans Serif
Modern typefaces that do not have decorative strokes (e.g., Montserrat).
